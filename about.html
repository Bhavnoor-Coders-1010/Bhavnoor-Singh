About Me

A quick introduction to who I am and what I've done.

I'm a final-year undergraduate student pursuing B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, with a CPI of 9.0/10.0. My academic journey began in Mechanical Engineering, but I earned a branch change to CSE based on strong first-year performance.

My research interests lie at the intersection of deep learning, brain-computer interfaces (BCI), and computer vision. I've worked on projects involving EEG signal processing, real-time BCI paradigms using OpenViBE, and deepfake detection systems. I enjoy applying ML/DL techniques to solve problems in neuroscience and healthcare.

Beyond academics, I'm deeply involved in the IIT Kanpur community. I serve as a Core Team Member of the Institute Counselling Service, supporting a campus of 4500+ students, and previously held the role of Secretary at the Brain and Cognitive Science Club, where I mentored juniors in ML and CNNs.

I'm driven by the desire to build things that matter — whether that's a finance tracking app that helps people manage their money, a BCI system that helps differently-abled individuals communicate, or an automated testing pipeline that improves software quality.
